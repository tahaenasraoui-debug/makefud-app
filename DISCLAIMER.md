- It is not medical, dietary or nutritional advice, and it does not replace consultation with a physician, registered dietitian or other qualified professional.

- MakeFud does not check recipes for allergens, intolerances or food safety. If you have an allergy, medical condition or special diet, verify every ingredient yourself.

### TheMealDB

TheMealDB ([themealdb.com](https://www.themealdb.com/api.php)) is not used by the current version. If a contributor adds it as a data source, the contribution must follow TheMealDB's published API terms, including their rules on the free test key, commercial use and attribution, and must credit them in this section and in the app footer. Note that TheMealDB does not provide calorie or preparation time data, so it cannot feed those sort options without another source.

The built-in demo recipes are original placeholder entries written for this project. Their times and calorie values are illustrative, not measured.
